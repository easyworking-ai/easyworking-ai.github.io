# About Working AI

## What this site covers

Working AI is a content site about the decisions that appear when AI is attached to real work.

It explains what AI can do, where it can fail, and what a person should check when using it for meeting notes, email, spreadsheets, or agent workflows.

## Content areas

- **Guides**: Prompts and review procedures for recurring work such as meeting notes, email, and spreadsheet analysis
- **Learning**: Practical explanations of chatbots, agents, RAG, and context windows
- **Radio**: Weekly AI stories connected to practical work situations
- **Experiments**: Interactive tools for comparing AI behavior and testing your own decisions
- **Practice notes**: Installation and usage records that separate verified results from plans and unresolved points

## Editorial standards

The site follows these principles:

1. Explain the work situation before naming a tool.
2. Show where a person must review an AI-generated result.
3. Add original sources and a check date when a page depends on current information or figures.
4. Separate what was run directly, what is planned, and what has not been verified.
5. Correct a page when an error is confirmed and record the change when useful.
