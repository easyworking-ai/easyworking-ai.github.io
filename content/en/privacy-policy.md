# Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 23, 2026

Working AI (EasyWorking-AI, “the site”) processes information only as needed to operate the website, understand how its content is used, and respond to requests. This page describes what may be processed when you use the public pages and interactive tools.

## 1. Information that may be processed

The site does not require an account. The following information may be processed during use:

- Technical information sent by a browser and server request, such as request time, requested address, browser and device information, and IP address
- Page views and visit-flow information collected through GoatCounter analytics
- An email address and optional name or job role when you choose to request publication alerts or save an AI work-readiness result
- Language and theme settings stored in your browser’s `localStorage`
- Information that you include directly in an inquiry email

Your email address and optional name or job role are submitted only when you choose to request a result, an alert, or send an inquiry. You can complete the diagnostic and view its result without entering an email address.

## 2. Purposes of use

Information may be used to:

- Check site errors and maintain security
- Understand which pages and tools are used so the content can be improved
- Send a publication alert or diagnostic result you requested
- Respond to correction, copyright, privacy, or collaboration requests

## 3. Interactive tools and email

Entering an email address in the AI work-readiness diagnostic is optional. If you check the consent box and submit the form, the email address, optional fields, and diagnostic result may be sent to a webhook endpoint operated at `aiee.app`. If the webhook request fails, the tool may open an email application using a `mailto:` link.

You can view the diagnostic result without entering an email address. To stop alerts or request deletion of information you submitted, contact the address below.

## 4. Analytics and external services

The site uses GoatCounter to understand page usage. The scope and retention of information processed by the analytics service depend on that service’s policy and configuration. When you submit a diagnostic result or alert request, the `aiee.app` webhook and your email application may receive the submitted values.

Fonts, equation rendering, interactive tools, and external links may cause a browser request to another service. When you leave the site or submit information to an external service, that service’s privacy policy applies.

## 5. Google AdSense and advertising cookies

The site does not currently display advertising. If Google AdSense is added later, Google and advertising partners may use or read cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar identifiers to provide advertising. Personalized advertising and consent handling may vary by AdSense settings and region.

If advertising is added, this policy and the site’s consent choices will be updated before or when the relevant advertising features are enabled.

## 6. Retention and deletion requests

Information is retained only for as long as needed for the purpose for which it was submitted, subject to the settings of external services and any legally required retention. To cancel an alert, request deletion, or ask a question about processing, use the address below.

## 7. External links

The site may link to official documents, research materials, and service pages. The operators of those external sites are responsible for their own processing practices.

## 8. Changes to this policy

This page will be updated when site features, analytics, or advertising practices change. The effective date will be shown at the top.
