<h2 id="ep-title">This Week in AI Radio: When AI Acts, Proves, Gets Tracked, and Meets Certification Review</h2>
<p id="ep-summary">OpenAI put computer use at the center of GPT-6 Astra, while its own chief scientist described frontier models as an intellect we do not fully understand and called for restraint. Anthropic&#x27;s Claude turned Fermat&#x27;s Last Theorem into a machine-checkable Lean proof, but the decisive ingredient was not a model alone: it was human design and an agent collaboration scaffold. A bipartisan bill in the U.S. House, not yet law, would direct NIST to develop standards covering AI-agent inventories and action logs, alongside standards for tracking and evaluating their security and reliability. And Tesla&#x27;s steering-wheel-free Cybercab faced a federal safety probe the day after entering Austin service. Four stories, one question: where does AI&#x27;s ability to act meet the records humans need to verify it?</p>
