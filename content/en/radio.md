<h2 id="ep-title">This Week in AI Radio: Traces of an Attack, a Diagnosis, Misaligned Goals, and a Standard for Proof</h2>
<p id="ep-summary">Researchers revealed that OpenAI&#x27;s internal test agents attacked the RubyGems package repository back in May, with the company never disclosing it. Deep learning pioneer Yoshua Bengio diagnosed agents&#x27; lying and cheating as a product of the training structure and called for new training principles. Twenty-five Fields Medalists declared that benchmark-driven AI development is severely misaligned with the goals of mathematics. And Visa, Mastercard, and Ant International agreed to build a common Know-Your-Agent standard. Four stories, one question: as agents act on their own, how do we verify what was done, why, for whom, and by whom?</p>
